A growing library of workbooks and resources, organised by topic

Astrophysics

1 resource
  • Reading the Light of the Universe — IB HL Astrophysics Guided Research

    A week-long guided research project in which each student picks a real star and pulls its own data from the SIMBAD and Gaia archives, then works through five stages — the thermal model (Wien's law, Stefan–Boltzmann, luminosity), the wave model (Doppler shift and Hubble's law), stellar equilibrium and fusion, evolution on the HR diagram, and distance/radius from parallax — before synthesising it all into an argument about the star's fate and the future of the Universe. Includes three assessment options: a written investigation, a comparative poster with oral defence, or a structured exam-style paper.

Collaborative Project

1 resource
  • Energy Balance & the Greenhouse Effect — Student Workbook

    A two-lesson workbook on radiation, emissivity, the solar constant and albedo, followed by Earth's energy balance and the greenhouse effect. Includes a synthesis task on real planetary energy budgets, an interactive inverse-square-law simulation, and the brief for the 10-hour Collaborative Sciences Project investigating albedo or foliage energy shielding, with an understanding checklist and self-marking answer key.

Currents and Circuits

1 resource
  • Current and Circuits — Student Workbook

    A complete, consolidated workbook covering the whole topic in one place: cells and circuit diagrams, charge and current, potential difference and Kirchhoff's laws, series and parallel circuits, conductors/insulators and the origin of resistance, resistance/Ohm's law and I–V characteristics, resistivity, combining resistors, emf and internal resistance, electrical power, and variable resistors and potentiometers, with a glossary.

Electromagnetism

4 resources
  • Electric and Magnetic Fields — Student Workbook

    Covers electric charge, charging and discharging by induction, Coulomb's law (with an interactive inverse-square-law simulation), and electric fields.

  • Millikan's Oil-Drop Experiment — Student Workbook

    An interactive workbook and simulation recreating Millikan's oil-drop experiment: balancing the forces on a charged drop, collecting simulated data, deducing the elementary charge from repeated measurements, and evaluating the experiment's assumptions and sources of uncertainty.

  • Motion in Electromagnetic Fields — Student Workbook

    Covers conventional current versus the flow of electrons, and the motion of charged particles in uniform electric and uniform magnetic fields, finishing with a "predict the path" quiz.

  • Electromagnetic Induction — Student Workbook

    Covers motional emf, magnetic flux and flux linkage, Faraday's law and Lenz's law, AC generators and the effect of frequency, and transformers (including a 3D transformer thermal simulation), with an equation summary and glossary.

Mechanics

6 resources
  • Kinematics — Student Workbook

    Covers position, distance and displacement, speed and velocity, acceleration, motion graphs, the SUVAT equations of motion, vertical motion under gravity, and projectile motion.

  • Forces — Student Workbook

    Covers forces as interactions, Newton's three laws of motion, and free-body diagrams.

  • Conservation of Momentum — Student Workbook

    Covers momentum and its conservation, collisions in one dimension, elastic and inelastic collisions, explosions, impulse, and Newton's second law revisited for systems with changing mass, with a glossary.

  • Work, Energy and Power — Student Workbook

    Covers work done by a force, energy and its conservation, kinetic and gravitational potential energy, and Hooke's law and elastic potential energy.

  • Gravitation and Circular Motion — Student Workbook

    Covers Newton's law of gravitation, gravitational field strength and field lines, gravitational potential energy and potential, circular motion, Kepler's laws of orbital motion, and orbital speed, escape speed and drag.

  • Simple Harmonic Motion — Student Workbook

    Covers the conditions for simple harmonic motion, describing SHM, the time period of a mass–spring system, the simple pendulum, energy changes during SHM, phase angle, and solving problems with the SHM data-booklet equations.

Nuclear Physics

3 resources
  • The History of Nuclear & Quantum Physics — Student Workbook

    Traces the development of nuclear and quantum physics from the Geiger–Marsden–Rutherford experiment through nuclear notation, isotopes, nuclear radius and the strong nuclear force, to emission/absorption spectra, photons, the Bohr model (HL), the photoelectric effect (with Einstein's equation), and Compton scattering.

  • Radioactivity & Half-Life — Student Workbook

    Covers what radioactivity is and how it's detected against background radiation, building up to decay processes and half-life calculations.

  • Binding Energy & Nuclear Stability — Student Workbook

    Covers binding energy and mass defect, and how they relate to nuclear stability.

Rotational Dynamics

1 resource
  • Rotational Dynamics — Student Workbook

    Covers torque, rotational equilibrium, describing rotational motion, the equations of motion for angular acceleration, moment of inertia, Newton's second law for rotation, and angular momentum.

Special Relativity

3 resources
  • Galilean Relativity — Student Workbook

    Covers reference frames, inertial frames and Galilean relativity, the Galilean transformation equations, and the velocity addition equation, with a glossary.

  • Special Relativity — Student Workbook

    Covers the postulates of special relativity, the Lorentz transformation equations, proper time and proper length, time dilation, length contraction, the relativity of simultaneity, and evidence from muon decay.

  • Space-Time Diagrams — Student Workbook

    Covers constructing and interpreting spacetime diagrams to represent events, worldlines and simultaneity in special relativity.

Thermal Physics

3 resources
  • Methods of Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers particles in solids, liquids and gases, temperature and thermal equilibrium, internal energy and phase change, the three ways to transfer thermal energy, conduction, convection, and radiation and black bodies.

  • Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers the heating curve, internal energy along the curve, specific heat capacity, specific latent heat, and putting it all together, with a glossary.

  • Energy Changes with Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers a model for a gas, the ideal gas law, the internal energy of an ideal gas, the first law of thermodynamics, four thermodynamic processes, heat engines and the Carnot cycle, and entropy and the second law.

Tools

4 resources
  • Accuracy and Precision — IB Physics Practice

    Self-marking practice distinguishing accuracy (closeness to the true value, affected by systematic error) from precision (repeatability, affected by random error and instrument resolution), using the classic dartboard illustration.

  • Scientific Notation (Standard Form) — IB Physics Practice

    Self-marking practice writing numbers in and out of scientific notation, calculating with standard form, and applying it to real IB Physics problems from Topic 1: Measurements and uncertainties. Includes a foundational task and an extension task with hints.

  • Significant Figures & Estimates — IB Physics Practice

    Self-marking practice on identifying and rounding to significant figures and carrying them through calculations, followed by a section on order-of-magnitude estimation for everyday quantities.

  • A-Level Uncertainty

    A saved reference page (via Dr Jones Physics) on calculating and combining measurement uncertainties, covering absolute, fractional and percentage uncertainty and how they propagate through calculations.

Waves

5 resources
  • The Nature of Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers what a wave is, transverse versus longitudinal waves, describing a wave (wavelength, amplitude, period), wave speed (v = fλ = λ/T), sound and electromagnetic waves, mechanical vs. electromagnetic waves, wavefronts and rays, and reflection at boundaries, with a glossary.

  • Velocity Change in Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers wavefronts and rays, wave behaviour at boundaries (reflection, transmission and refraction), why refraction happens, wavefront-ray diagrams of refraction, refractive index, Snell's law, and critical angle and total internal reflection.

  • Interference of Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers diffraction, superposition of waves and pulses, coherence and interference patterns, path difference and the conditions for interference, Young's double-slit experiment, single-slit diffraction, and how single-slit diffraction modulates the double-slit pattern.

  • Standing Waves and Resonance — Student Workbook

    Covers the formation of standing waves, nodes and antinodes, standing wave patterns on strings and in pipes, natural frequency and resonance, and damping, with a glossary.

  • From a Passing Siren to an Expanding Universe — IB HL Guided Investigation

    A three-part guided investigation (Topic C.5): the Doppler effect for sound and light with an interactive wavefront simulator, red shift and the comparison of spectra with a linked external redshift simulator, and an open investigation into the expanding Universe using an elastic-band model and real galaxy recession data, finishing with a written report or investigation journal.
