IB DP Physics

A growing library of workbooks and resources, organised by topic

Astrophysics

1 resource
  • Reading the Light of the Universe — IB HL Astrophysics Guided Research

    A week-long guided research project in which students use real star data from SIMBAD and Gaia to determine a star's temperature, motion, structure and evolutionary stage, then argue for its fate and the Universe's future.

Collaborative Project

1 resource
  • Energy Balance & the Greenhouse Effect — Student Workbook

    A two-lesson workbook on radiation, albedo and the greenhouse effect that leads into a 10-hour Collaborative Sciences Project investigating albedo or foliage energy shielding.

Currents and Circuits

1 resource
  • Current and Circuits — Student Workbook

    A consolidated workbook covering the whole circuits topic, from charge and current through Kirchhoff's laws and resistance to emf, power and potentiometers.

Electromagnetism

4 resources
  • Electric and Magnetic Fields — Student Workbook

    Covers electric charge, charging and discharging, Coulomb's law and electric fields, with an interactive inverse-square-law simulation.

  • Millikan's Oil-Drop Experiment — Student Workbook

    An interactive simulation of Millikan's oil-drop experiment in which students balance forces on charged drops to deduce the elementary charge and evaluate the method's uncertainties.

  • Motion in Electromagnetic Fields — Student Workbook

    Covers conventional current versus electron flow and the motion of charged particles in uniform electric and magnetic fields, finishing with a predict-the-path quiz.

  • Electromagnetic Induction — Student Workbook

    Covers motional emf, flux linkage, Faraday's and Lenz's laws, AC generators and transformers, including a 3D transformer thermal simulation.

Mechanics

6 resources
  • Kinematics — Student Workbook

    Covers position, velocity, acceleration, motion graphs, the SUVAT equations, and vertical and projectile motion.

  • Forces — Student Workbook

    Covers forces as interactions, Newton's three laws of motion, and free-body diagrams.

  • Conservation of Momentum — Student Workbook

    Covers momentum conservation, collisions, explosions, impulse, and Newton's second law for systems with changing mass.

  • Work, Energy and Power — Student Workbook

    Covers work, energy conservation, kinetic and gravitational potential energy, and Hooke's law and elastic potential energy.

  • Gravitation and Circular Motion — Student Workbook

    Covers Newton's law of gravitation, gravitational fields and potential, circular motion, Kepler's laws, and orbital and escape speed.

  • Simple Harmonic Motion — Student Workbook

    Covers the conditions for SHM, mass-spring and pendulum systems, energy changes, phase angle, and the SHM data-booklet equations.

Nuclear Physics

3 resources
  • The History of Nuclear & Quantum Physics — Student Workbook

    Traces nuclear and quantum physics from the Rutherford experiment through atomic spectra and the Bohr model to the photoelectric effect and Compton scattering.

  • Radioactivity & Half-Life — Student Workbook

    Covers what radioactivity is, how it's detected, and decay and half-life calculations.

  • Binding Energy & Nuclear Stability — Student Workbook

    Covers binding energy, mass defect, and their link to nuclear stability.

Rotational Dynamics

1 resource
  • Rotational Dynamics — Student Workbook

    Covers torque, rotational equilibrium, angular motion equations, moment of inertia, Newton's second law for rotation, and angular momentum.

Special Relativity

3 resources
  • Galilean Relativity — Student Workbook

    Covers reference frames, inertial frames, the Galilean transformation equations, and velocity addition.

  • Special Relativity — Student Workbook

    Covers the postulates of special relativity, the Lorentz transformations, time dilation, length contraction, simultaneity, and evidence from muon decay.

  • Space-Time Diagrams — Student Workbook

    Covers constructing and interpreting spacetime diagrams to represent events, worldlines and simultaneity.

Thermal Physics

3 resources
  • Methods of Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers particle models of matter, thermal equilibrium, internal energy, and conduction, convection and radiation.

  • Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers the heating curve, specific heat capacity, and specific latent heat.

  • Energy Changes with Heating — Student Workbook

    Covers the ideal gas model, the first law of thermodynamics, thermodynamic processes, heat engines, and entropy.

Waves

5 resources
  • The Nature of Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers wave types, describing waves, wave speed, sound and electromagnetic waves, and wavefronts, rays and reflection.

  • Velocity Change in Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers reflection, refraction, refractive index, Snell's law, and total internal reflection.

  • Interference of Waves — Student Workbook

    Covers diffraction, superposition, coherence, path difference, and Young's double-slit and single-slit diffraction.

  • Standing Waves and Resonance — Student Workbook

    Covers the formation of standing waves, nodes and antinodes, resonance, and damping.

  • From a Passing Siren to an Expanding Universe — A Guided Investigation

    A three-part investigation into the Doppler effect, red shift and spectral comparison, and the expanding Universe, using an interactive simulator and real galaxy data.
